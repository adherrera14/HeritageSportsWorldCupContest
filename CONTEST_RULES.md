# Contest Rules

This guide explains the basic format of the contest for a new player.

## Overview

Before the tournament starts, each player ranks all 48 World Cup teams from strongest to weakest.

- `48` = the team you believe is the strongest
- `1` = the team you believe is the weakest

As matches are played, you earn points when the teams you backed win.

## How To Enter

1. Review all 48 teams.
2. Assign every team one unique ranking value from `48` down to `1`.
3. Use each number only once.
4. Submit your rankings before the tournament begins.

Once your rankings are submitted, they are locked and cannot be changed.

## Scoring

You score points based on the ranking number you assigned to a team.

- If your team wins a match, you earn that team's ranking value in points.
- If a match ends in a draw, you earn `0` points for that match.
- If your team loses, you earn `0` points for that match.

### Example

- You rank Brazil as `48`.
- Brazil wins a match.
- You earn `48` points.

If that same team keeps winning later in the tournament, you keep earning that same ranking value for each win.

## Tournament Progression

The contest follows the tournament from start to finish:

1. Pre-Tournament
2. Group Stage - Matchday 1
3. Group Stage - Matchday 2
4. Group Stage - Matchday 3
5. Round of 32
6. Round of 16
7. Quarterfinal
8. Semifinal
9. Final

Your total score continues to build throughout the event as long as your ranked teams keep winning.

## Strategy Notes

- Giving a team a high number increases the points you earn if that team wins.
- Because each number can only be used once, every ranking decision matters.
- Picking the eventual deep tournament runners correctly can create a large scoring advantage.

## Quick Summary

- Rank all 48 teams from `48` to `1`
- Every number must be unique
- Submit once before the tournament starts
- Rankings lock after submission
- Points are awarded only for wins
- Total score is the sum of all points earned across the tournament
